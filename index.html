Echoes of Reverie (09/2025)

Emerging from a creative pause, Echoes of Reverie marks a contemplative new chapter for Back Down Scenario. This single invites listeners into a soundscape where time expands, and melodies unfold with deliberate grace, creating space for reflection and emotional resonance.

Building on the atmospheric foundation of Journey, this composition trades riffs for expansive, flowing arrangements. The track's unhurried pace allows each note to breathe, crafting an immersive experience that encourages listeners to lose themselves in its rich textures and evolving harmonies.

True to the project's diverse roots, Echoes of Reverie weaves together elements from Back Down Scenario's entire musical journey. The result is a piece that feels both familiar and fresh—a perfect reentry point that honors the past while looking forward to new creative horizons.

Journey (10/2020)

Journey represents an evolution of sound that transcends time, weaving together five years of musical exploration into a cohesive narrative. This album captures the essence of transformation, blending atmospheric textures with melodic depth to create an immersive listening experience that resonates on multiple levels.

Drawing from the creative well of 2015-2019, these tracks showcase a refined musical direction while honoring the project's melodic roots. The result is a collection that feels both familiar and refreshingly new—a testament to the artist's growth and willingness to explore new sonic territories while staying true to their core identity.

Broken Shadow (10/2019)

Broken Shadow navigates life's complex emotional landscape, masterfully balancing intensity with moments of serene reflection. This third release delivers a powerful musical journey through adversity, where raw energy meets melodic sophistication.

Rooted in the creative period of 2005-2013, these tracks showcase a compelling evolution in songwriting. The EP's foundation in rock and metal is enriched with progressive elements, creating a dynamic tension between darkness and light.

While maintaining the signature intensity of Back to Reality, this collection introduces a more nuanced approach to composition. The result is a deeply atmospheric experience that ultimately resolves in an uplifting blend of warmth and resilience.

Back to Reality (11/2017)

Marking a bold step forward from the debut, Back to Reality embraces a grittier rock and metal aesthetic while maintaining the emotional depth that defined A Gate to the Past. This EP serves as a natural progression, expanding the musical vocabulary with greater intensity and complexity.

Drawn from the creative well of 2007-2008, these tracks showcase an artist pushing boundaries. The inclusion of When Rain Starts Falling highlights a growing progressive influence, adding new dimensions to the project's evolving sound.

Tracks like Enlightened Path serve as a bridge between releases, demonstrating how the project's musical DNA connects across albums. The result is a collection that feels both cohesive and exploratory, offering listeners a deeper dive into the artist's creative vision.

A Gate to the Past (02/2017)

The first album offers a glimpse into the early beginnings of the project. The songs were written between 2005 and 2007 and were finally composed and recorded in autumn and winter 2016. In early 2017, Back Down Scenario therefore saw its first public appearance, although it had been developed piece by piece behind closed doors for many years.

Stylistic variations of melodic compositions, rock and partly metal influences, as well as melancholic to cheerful themes form the tension between the songs on the album. All in all a relaxed and supporting atmosphere around the beginnings of the project is created with versatile and varied sounds and impressions.
